# Colmado

**The neighborhood shop, online.**

[colmado.click](https://colmado.click) · New York

</div>

---

Colmado gives bodegas and corner stores online orders and payments: pickup, delivery and in-store, by card or digital dollars, paid straight to the shop. Monthly memberships and prepaid plans are built in, in English and Spanish.

Colmado is operated by SebasTN Inc., founded and led by [Rensley R.](https://github.com/doble196). Company page: [github.com/SebasTN-Inc](https://github.com/SebasTN-Inc).
